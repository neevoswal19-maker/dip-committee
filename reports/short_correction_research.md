# Short market corrections: backtest

Generated 2026-10-30 in 3 minutes. Nifty 500 from 2005-09-26 to 2026-10-30; 481 stock histories (today's Nifty 500, dividend-adjusted).

**Rules (src/strategy/correction.py, fixed before running):** a correction is the Nifty 500 closing 5%, 7% or 10% below its highest close of the last 3, 6 or 12 months. It ends when the index closes back within half that fall of its window high; only then can the next one start. Buy every stock that fell further than the index from its own high over the same window, equal money each (Rs 5 lakh basket), at the next open; sell at that high or after six months. No stop. Groww delivery charges and slippage. The index trade buys at the same open and sells when the index regains its peak, or after six months, with no costs. Episodes can overlap.

**Pass criteria:** at least 10 completed episodes; the basket beats the index trade in two-thirds of them and on average; at least 60% of trades reach their high within six months; and the basket beats buying every stock in the universe at the same moment, on average. That last check was added after the first correction study showed today's index members beat the index on their own (survivorship).
